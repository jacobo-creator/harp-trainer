How the technique hint works

Pick your harmonica's key above. As you play, the app shows which hole and breath you're hitting — +4 = blow 4, -4 = draw 4, and apostrophes mark bends (-3' = ½-step bend, -3'' = whole-step). Watch the needle to train your bends to the right pitch.
